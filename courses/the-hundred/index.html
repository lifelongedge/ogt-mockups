The Hundred — a trading business that writes itself

The 100 essays become 100 pages of a book the trader fills in, inside Edge S2 Desktop. A page is read, then done, and what is done is kept: a written artifact in the trader's own Binder. Pages open when they are earned — by an artifact, by a date, or by evidence in the trader's own Journal — and some pages open themselves on the day the Journal shows the trader needs them. The free edition is the placement and the tips at a glance; the paid edition is the book with its automation.

Two other framings, for the record

The Dojo

Ritual-first: the daily stand-to and the score are the product, the 100 pages are kata earned by practising. Stronger daily habit, weaker for the Planning and Assessment pages that are written work.

The Ladder

Stage-first: placement prescribes the next five pages across disciplines, nothing else is visible. Simplest screen, but hides the shape of the whole and the event pages have nowhere to appear.

Why the Book. The essays already carry a practice, a test and two guards each; the Book keeps the essays' own structure and adds only three things the desktop can do that a web page cannot: read the Journal, keep a date, and hold an artifact.

6Decisions this mockup needs

1 · The name and the ladder

The Hundred with Novice→Master (the tips' own ladder), or Ken's ladder from the 2023-10-29 Foundations Q&A: herd · hobbyist · gambler · novice · journeyman · meister · artist. The tips were written on the first; the second is on tape.

2 · How hard the locks are

Locks as shown (earned), locks as suggestions (everything readable, Do and Keep gated), or no locks and the Binder alone shows progress. The mockup argues for earned locks on Do/Keep with every essay readable.

3 · Edition and price

Part of the Owl Methods pack, a product of its own in the store, or the Foundations course's sequel. The videos decide the build cost: 100 animated explainers at the s2-intro rate is the long pole.

4 · The six event pages

Pages that open themselves on a Journal event (cold streak, revenge re-entry, +5R day, 10% drawdown, three stop-moves, drift). Owner's call whether the book may interrupt a trading day, or only surface them at the next stand-to.

5 · Ken's voice

Whose tips these are is not recorded in the source file. If they are Ken's, the Read beat can say so; if not, the videos are OGT's voice with "Ken on…" clips kept apart, as the Chapter 3 pilot did.

6 · The store rework

Topicals are parked behind store v2 (2026-07-13). This is a product, not a topical, but the same hands build it. Sequence call.
