2.2 The Dragon: the noise boundary

Skill: see the Dragon as the edge of noise, and RL10 crossing it as the event

The Dragon is a 10-bar average of RL10 with a skin and a belly half a sigma either side. Inside the band, movement is noise. RL10 leaving the band is the crossing that every SSC and Kata-2 pattern is built on, and the second of the five points in the Owl setup.

Owl Group reading. Platform definition: dragon → edge-transform calc_dragon. Guide ›

Try it. With the future hidden, find the most recent bar where RL10 crossed up through the Dragon. Click that bar on the chart.
Your pick: none yet. Click a bar.
A crossing is a description of past bars. This lesson does not say what happens next.

3A frame lesson: 3.3 The trade frame, both directions

Risk geometry is the part of the method most students get wrong, so the app does the arithmetic and the student does the reading. The Frog lines are drawn from today's open in units of 1R; the frame overlays entry, initial stop and target for the direction chosen. The Prepare step's six questions are answered for both directions from the same bars, and the R:R gate colours itself. The check is a read: which direction passes the gate, and what is xR in Frogs? Step the slider and watch the answer change.

3.3 The trade frame, both directions

Skill: size in 1R (the Frog), execute at xR (the pivot), and let the gate decide

1R is a risk box built from the symbol's own daily ranges. The method has several (Frog, RangeStat, ATR); Foundations uses the 30-day Frog. xR is the distance from entry to the execution stop at a local pivot. The frame has no opinion: entry a nickel past yesterday's extreme, stop a nickel past the other extreme, target the 10-day extreme. Reward-to-risk below 2.0 is rejected.

Owl Group reading of frame, frog. Frame arithmetic is display arithmetic (TD-003), not canonical math.

Try it. Which direction passes the gate today, and how many Frogs is xR? Say it, then press Check.
A frame is a falsifiable structure. It is not a recommendation to take either side.

4A checklist lesson and a drill: 4.3 The Owl setup, 4.4 Decide before the reveal

The Owl setup's five points are each a computable statement about past bars, so the app evaluates all five at the as-of bar and shows which hold. The D3A drill sits on top: the student steps one bar at a time (Detect), commits to Long / Short / Sit out (Decide) before pressing ▶ (Deliver), and the log scores agreement with the checklist, never P&L (Assess). The slider is parked on IWM in early August 2025. Step back a few bars to watch the points switch on one by one.

5.4 Zero State and Operation FIG

Skill: arrive at the screen centred, and leave the last trade behind you

Zero State is the baseline you trade from: unhurried, cleared of anxiety and of magical thinking. The Owl archetype is the reminder. Quiet observation, patience without a deadline, awareness without judgement. The method holds that execution is decoupled from personal P&L, and Zero State is what makes that possible in a body wired for fight or flight.

Operation FIG is the daily boundary routine that gets you there. Three lines, spoken or written, before the first chart opens.

ForgivenessRelease yesterday's losses and mistakes. They were data, and the data has been logged.
IntentionSet today's execution identity. "I am the operator who follows the frame."
GratitudeFrame market feedback as information you were given, not a verdict on you.

The Owl meditation extends this into a short structured visualisation. It is offered in the pack as text; the app plays no audio and keeps no timer.

Attributed to the Owl Group method. This lesson has no chart and no check, and the app will not invent one.

6How a lesson is authored: the course file and the check kinds

Coursecraft writes lessons as YAML; the app supplies the mechanism. This is the SD-011 shape from docs/method-packs-plan.md §5, extended with the check kinds the sheets above need and a fixture per computable check so s2desktop can test its engine against a pinned answer. No video in this course, by owner direction.

packs/method/owl/courses/foundations.yaml (excerpt)

Three rules carried from the platform

  • The future is hidden on every marked check. A lesson cannot be passed on a live chart.
  • Every number is computed from the student's own store and prints its window and sample size.
  • Prose is attributed to the method. A lesson says what the structure is and what Owl Group does about it, and stops there.

8Phased plan and the handoff boundary

Coursecraft ships a course bundle: prose, readings, check definitions and pinned fixtures. S2desktop ships the engine: loader, overlays, check kinds, Learn tab, journal, installer. The boundary is a versioned bundle schema and a fixtures file; content moves across it by a handoff issue, never by editing the other repo. Full plan: ogt-coursecraft/courses/owl-foundations/plan.md.
