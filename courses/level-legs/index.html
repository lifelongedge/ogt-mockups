Between Two Levels

Course concept, mockup with live data. The same two levels, say the open to +1 Frog, or −7 R10 to −8 R10, are joined by very different paths. The measures we already draw (RL10, the dragon, the mean, z1, z2) name each path. Every leg below is a real one, from our own 1-minute bars.

Seven shapes of the same move

Shape map

Every leg in the filter, by how long it took and how straight it went. Straightness is the net move over the distance travelled, close to close (1 is a straight line). Hover a dot; press one with a ring to open its chart.

The shapes in numbers

Medians over the legs in the filter. Descriptive of what these legs did; nothing here says what the next leg will do.

What the course would teach

  1. The rulers. R10, R5 and Frog from the last 30 sessions, laid off from the open. Why each ruler gets its own bar size: R10 on 1 and 3 minutes, R5 on 3 and 10, Frog on 10 and 30.
  2. A leg. The last touch of one level to the first touch of the next. Inside a leg price never goes back to where it started, so the whole picture lives between two lines.
  3. Reading the path. Where the closes sit against the dragon spine and skin, which way RL10 points, how often the mean is crossed, how far the z bands are stretched.
  4. The seven shapes. One chapter each, with today's legs beside the old ones.
  5. Your own tape. The same measures on the trader's own symbols in Edge S2, so a shape is named while it forms.
Open for the owner. The shape names and the cut-offs that sort a leg into one (shown on each card) are a first draft written to make the spread visible. Whether any shape is followed by anything in particular has not been measured; this page only describes the legs.
