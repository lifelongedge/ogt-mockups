7 · Open decisions
  1. Which variant. A gives a reason to buy a second course (it doubles the credit) and stops at a floor of $1,997 for coaching. B is the simplest to explain. C adds a cheaper first step but needs a new $24.97 product.
  2. The cap. A shows $1,000. Without one, five courses would take coaching down to about $500.
  3. Past buyers. About 18 people bought courses, some at $19.97 or $29.97. Count their purchases (10× what they paid), or start the credit from launch day.
  4. Observer tier ($997 a year). Credit on Active only, or also on Observer with a lower cap (for example $300).
  5. Rules that hold in every variant: only money paid counts (gifts such as Part 2 for past buyers earn nothing), a refund removes its credit, one year to use it, applied automatically at the coaching checkout by the buyer's email.
