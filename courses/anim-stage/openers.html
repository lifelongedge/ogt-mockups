Opener options · the first five seconds of every Owl Group Trading course

What the Stanford videos do: one bright full-frame picture, the lesson title in a frosted box low in the frame, the presenter top-right, held for 7–12 seconds, then the black stage. These three are drawn in code (deterministic, so the kit renders them frame by frame); the owl is the brand archetype, never a stock image. Scrub or play; the title box and the tile are in every option.

Option A

First light

Golden hour over rolling ridges, the sun just up, a long mist in the valley; the owl on a fence post at the right, watching. The light is the brand: patient, early, awake before the market.

Moves: the sun lifts, the mist drifts, the far ridges breathe, the owl blinks once; the title box fades in at 1.2 s, the wordmark at 2.0 s. Warm palette on the brand amber; the tile keeps its corner.
Option B

The watch

Blue hour at the shore, a lighthouse sweeping, the owl on a bollard at the water's edge. Cooler and quieter; the beam says the same thing the Zero State says: nothing moves until it is seen.

Moves: the beam sweeps once every four seconds, the water shimmers, a few stars, the owl turns its head to the beam; title at 1.2 s. Works best for the system and risk courses.
Option C

Above the valley

Full daylight: big cumulus, a green valley with a river, the owl gliding across the frame on open wings with its shadow on the land. The most Stanford-like in brightness; the most motion of the three.

Moves: the owl crosses right to left over six seconds with a slow wingbeat, the clouds drift, the river glints; title at 1.2 s. Works for the open-and-intraday courses.
